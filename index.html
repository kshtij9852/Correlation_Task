All correlations are computed on day-over-day changes (ΔM, ΔS, ΔFly, ΔDFly, ΔCL, ΔBW), not price levels, since level correlations are dominated by shared trend rather than genuine co-movement. Spread(i) defaults to Mi − Mi+1; Fly(i) defaults to Spread(i) − Spread(i+1); DFly(i) defaults to Fly(i) − Fly(i+1). Override the second leg on any two-leg structure to build a non-adjacent version, e.g. DFly(1, 3) = Fly(1) − Fly(3). Brent–WTI Spread(i) = Brent Mi − WTI CLi by default (second leg overrides the WTI month). This tool is descriptive/statistical, not investment advice.
